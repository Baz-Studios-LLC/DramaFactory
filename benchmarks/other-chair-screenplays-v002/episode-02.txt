THE OTHER CHAIR
EPISODE 02 — TEN MINUTES
Assistant-authored development draft
Target: 60–90 seconds, to be measured with speech and acting. Continues directly from episode 01.

INT. SLOANE'S SECONDHAND — AFTERNOON

Nora holds her phone. Leah still holds Emma's twenty-dollar bill.

BEN — PHONE
Nora?

NORA
I heard you. Can I call you back?

BEN — PHONE
Yeah. Of course.

She ends the call. Looks relieved. Then notices Leah noticing.

NORA
You wanted half.

LEAH
You wanted to change the subject. Look at us, negotiating.

Nora takes the bill. Tears a receipt from Leah's pad, draws a line down the middle.

NORA
I do the talking. You do the chairs.

LEAH
And the rent. And the electricity. And explaining why a bride is shouting at a woman she met eight minutes ago.

NORA
Ten minutes. Twenty dollars. Half each.

Leah gets a pen. Writes a heading: RULES.

LEAH
No filming in here.

NORA
Absolutely not.

LEAH
No telling people what anyone said.

NORA
Even the good bits?

Leah waits. Nora draws a tiny, regretful line through GOOD BITS.

NORA
Fine.

LEAH
And what are we actually selling?

NORA
A chance to practice.

LEAH
Not a happy ending.

NORA
I'm good. I'm not that good.

Leah slides ten dollars back to Nora. A partnership, small enough to fit under two coffee cups.

Nora writes OTHER CHAIR beneath the heading. Her phone rings. EMMA.

NORA
She only just left.

LEAH
No happy endings. Put it on speaker.

Nora looks up.

Leah catches herself. Pushes the phone gently back toward her.

LEAH
Ask first.

Nora answers privately.

NORA
Emma?

EMMA — PHONE
I'm outside her house.

CUT.
