THE OTHER CHAIR
EPISODE 04 — THE EMPTY SATURDAY
Assistant-authored development draft
Target: 1–2 minutes. Mostly 1–4-second coverage; measure after speech and acting. Continues later on day one, after Emma's call.

INT. SLOANE'S SECONDHAND — LATE AFTERNOON

The shop bell rings. Emma comes back in, phone in hand. Nora is already pulling out a chair.

EMMA
She might not come.

NORA
Sit down. We can—

Emma stays standing.

EMMA
Do it better?

Nora stops moving the chair.

EMMA
I said what I meant. It just feels horrible.

NORA
You could tell her—

LEAH
Nora.

Leah sets down her receipt pad. Comes around the counter, empty-handed.

LEAH
What did you want Saturday to be like?

EMMA
I wanted to eat the expensive food. Dance with Jamie. Not spend the photos pulling my dress up.

She catches herself doing it now. Lets go of her neckline.

EMMA
I also wanted my mum there.

LEAH
Both can be true.

Emma unlocks her phone. Types: YOU'RE RIGHT, I DIDN'T MEAN—

Deletes it.

EMMA
If I leave her a place, is that giving in?

Nora looks at Leah. This time she has no line ready.

LEAH
Do you want her to have one?

Emma nods. Types a new message.

INSERT — PHONE: I LOVE YOU. YOUR PLACE IS THERE IF YOU WANT IT. I'M KEEPING THE DRESS.

She reads it once. Sends. Puts the phone in her bag before it can answer.

EMMA
Jamie booked us a tasting. I'm going to eat something.

She leaves. The empty chair is still pulled out.

Nora reaches for Emma's receipt on the counter.

NORA
Should we give her the twenty back?

Leah glances at the two chairs. The handwritten rules.

LEAH
Did we sell her mum coming?

Nora looks toward the door.

NORA
I think I thought we did.

CUT.
