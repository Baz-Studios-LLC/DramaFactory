THE OTHER CHAIR
EPISODE 06 — A BOX FOR TWO
Assistant-authored development draft
Target: 1–2 minutes. Mostly 1–4-second coverage with room for the final pause; measure after speech and acting. Day one, approaching shop closing.

INT. SLOANE'S SECONDHAND — LATE AFTERNOON

Ben enters sideways with three flattened packing boxes. Clips the doorbell with one corner. Catches the boxes before they hit a lamp.

BEN
I was going for effortless.

Nora takes the smallest box. Kisses him. Their ease is real.

NORA
Very convincing.

Ben spreads a printed apartment floor plan on a display table. His finger finds a small rectangle by a window.

BEN
That could be yours. Desk. Good light.

NORA
You measured for me?

BEN
For the desk. I know how big you are.

She bumps his shoulder with hers. He smiles, then looks at the plan again.

BEN
I haven't reserved anything.

Nora smooths a crease that does not need smoothing.

NORA
The van?

BEN
The van. The flat. This isn't a surprise move.

NORA
I know.

BEN
I don't think I told you properly. About the job.

She looks up.

BEN
Last year I let the training place go. I wanted to stay near you. That was my choice.

NORA
You never said.

BEN
I kept saying it wasn't the right time. Eventually you believe yourself.

His finger leaves the plan.

BEN
I want this one. I want a classroom that feels like mine.

Nora studies him: a person with an ambition, not just a man offering her a future.

NORA
And you want me there.

BEN
Very much.

He leaves a small space after it. Does not fill it for her.

NORA
Can we talk when the shop closes? Properly?

BEN
Tonight?

NORA
Tonight.

He nods. Folds the plan and hands it to her instead of leaving it open as an answer.

Ben goes to help Leah move a table. Nora unfolds the paper once more.

Two names written in his neat handwriting at the top. One of them is hers.

CUT.
