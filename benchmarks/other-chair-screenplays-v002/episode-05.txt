THE OTHER CHAIR
EPISODE 05 — ONE MORE FAVOR
Assistant-authored development draft
Target: 1–2 minutes. Mostly 1–4-second coverage; measure after speech and acting. Same afternoon. Lily is a new adult supporting role, not an approved visual casting.

INT. SLOANE'S SECONDHAND — DISPLAY LIVING ROOM — LATE AFTERNOON

LILY, 24, still in her cafe apron, sits opposite Nora. A takeaway cup rests on the empty chair beside her; Leah silently moves it onto a coaster.

LILY
I can't cover Saturday because my aunt's having surgery.

NORA — AS MANAGER
I'm sorry. Is she all right?

LILY
She's fictional.

Nora breaks character.

LILY
My real aunt would post from a vineyard and ruin it.

LEAH
Your family sounds supportive.

Lily almost laughs. Looks back at her phone.

LILY
He says I'm the only one he can rely on.

NORA
Were you available when he hired you?

LILY
Weekdays. I said weekdays.

NORA
Then try that.

LILY
I'm not available Saturday.

NORA — AS MANAGER
What are you doing?

LILY
Nothing.

She winces. Nora waits rather than rescuing her.

LILY
No. I'm not available Saturday.

A beat. It is harder without the aunt.

LILY
He's going to ask everyone else now.

LEAH
That is quite a lot of his job.

Lily types. Nora starts to lean across to see, then sits back.

LILY
It looks rude without the little smile.

Her thumb hovers over the keyboard.

She SENDS instead. Immediately puts the phone facedown.

LILY
There. Before I volunteer for Sunday too.

Nora smiles. Her own phone lights up beside Lily's.

INSERT — BEN: CAN I RESERVE THE VAN? NEED A DATE.

Lily notices Nora reading.

LILY
Yours too?

Nora's thumb reaches automatically for a smiling reply. Stops.

NORA
No. He's lovely.

She turns the phone facedown beside Lily's.

CUT.
