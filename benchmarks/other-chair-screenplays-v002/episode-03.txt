THE OTHER CHAIR
EPISODE 03 — THE FIRST CALL
Assistant-authored development draft
Target: 60–90 seconds, to be measured. Emma's mother may remain voice-only in this episode; do not promote an unused portrait study.

EXT. EMMA'S MOTHER'S HOUSE — LATE AFTERNOON

Emma stands at the garden gate. Her thumb presses the doorbell without touching it.

INTERCUT: NORA, at the furniture shop, phone to her ear. Leah moves out of earshot.

EMMA
I was going to go in.

NORA
Do you want to?

EMMA
I want this to be over.

Nora starts a reassuring answer. Stops.

NORA
Those aren't always the same thing.

Emma steps back from the bell. A long breath. She scrolls to MUM.

EMMA
Okay. I'm calling her.

NORA
You don't have to tell me how it goes.

Emma ends the call. Dials.

MOTHER — PHONE
I've found a seamstress. She thinks we can still fix the neckline.

Emma looks at the house. Nobody inside knows she is here.

EMMA
I'm keeping my dress.

MOTHER — PHONE
We discussed this.

EMMA
You discussed it.

That came out sharper than she intended. She almost apologizes. Her free hand opens.

EMMA
I want you at the wedding. I don't want you commenting on my body.

MOTHER — PHONE
I can't say anything now?

EMMA
You can say I look happy.

No answer. Emma finds herself smiling, as if she can make the sentence nicer through the phone. Lets the smile go.

MOTHER — PHONE
If that's how you feel about me, maybe I shouldn't come.

Emma grips the gate. She could be through the door in five seconds.

EMMA
I want you there.

She waits. Does not add BUT IT'S FINE, DOESN'T MATTER, I'M SORRY.

MOTHER — PHONE
I'll have to think about it.

The call ends. Emma stays outside. The house has not changed. Everything feels worse.

INT. SLOANE'S SECONDHAND — CONTINUOUS

Nora watches her own silent phone. Resists checking in.

Leah, from the far end of the room:

LEAH
Was it a happy ending?

NORA
I don't know.

The answer surprises her.

CUT.
